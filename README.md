# VORA

*Software development for Angola's oil & gas industry.*

---

We build systems that actually work in the field — dashboards that load on offshore connections, inspection apps that sync offline, and integrations that stop the endless spreadsheet ping-pong.

Our focus is the oil & gas ecosystem in Angola. Not as consultants. As builders.

---

## What we're doing

**Current focus:**
- Operational dashboards (real-time production, equipment status)
- Field inspection tooling (mobile-first, works offline)
- System integrations (legacy systems → modern APIs)

**On the roadmap:**
- Predictive maintenance models
- Asset tracking (equipment, vehicles, supply chain)
- Compliance & documentation portals
